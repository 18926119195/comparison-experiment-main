[Vamp Building]
0. The Vamp Building is a historic factory building at 3-15 Liberty Square in downtown Lynn, Massachusetts.
1.  The eight story brick building was built in 1903 to a design by local architect Henry Warren Rogers, and was extended over the next four years to occupy the entire city block bounded by Washington Street, Union Street, and Liberty Square.
2.  The "flatiron" V-shape of the building may have been a reason for its name.
3.  The building served in its early years as a home for all manner of businesses related to the manufacture of shoes.

[Freedom Square, Yerevan]
0. The Freedom Square or Liberty Square (Armenian: Ազատության հրապարակ, "Azatut'yan hraparak" ), also known as Opera Square and Theatre Square (Թատերական հրապարակ, "T'aterakan hraparak") until 1991, is a town square located in Kentron (Center) district of Yerevan, Armenia.
1.  The square is part of the Yerevan Opera Theater complex, located just to the south of the main opera building, between the opera park and the Swan lake.
2.  Along with the Republic Square, the Freedom Square is one of the two main squares in central Yerevan.
3.  It is bordered with four streets: Tumanyan Street, Teryan Street, Sayat Nova Avenue and Mashtots Avenue.
4.  The statues of writer Hovhannes Tumanyan and composer Alexander Spendiaryan are located in the square.

[Mark Twain Riverboat]

[Passyunk Square, Philadelphia]
0. Passyunk Square is a neighborhood in South Philadelphia bounded by Broad Street to the west, 6th Street to the east, Tasker Street to the south and Washington Avenue to the north.
1.  Passyunk Square is bordered by the Bella Vista, Hawthorne, Central South Philadelphia, Wharton and Point Breeze neighborhoods.
2.  The neighborhood got its Lenape name from the 1800s Passyunk Township, Pennsylvania which named Passyunk Square Park, located between 12th, 13th, Reed and Wharton Streets.
3.  The park was eventually renamed Columbus Square Park, and subsequently the neighborhood became known as Columbus Square.
4.  Geoff DiMasi revived the Passyunk Square name when forming the Passyunk Square Civic Association in 2003.

[15 Temmuz Kızılay Milli İrade Meydanı]
0. July 15th Kızılay National Will Square, originally Kızılay Square (Turkish: "Kızılay Meydanı"), is one of the most important centers and junction points of Ankara, Turkey.
1.  After the 27 May 1960 coup d'état it was formally renamed as Hürriyet Meydanı (Liberty Square) but the popular use of "Kızılay Meydanı" continued.
2.  Following the failed coup attempt of 15 July 2016, Ankara Metropolitan Municipality initially decided on 26 July 2016 to rename the square as 15 Temmuz Kızılay Demokrasi Meydanı (July 15th Kızılay Democracy Square), which was later amended as 15 Temmuz Kızılay Milli İrade Meydanı (July 15th Kızılay National Will Square) on 9 August 2016.
