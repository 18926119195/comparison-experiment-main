[Jones Beach Island]
0. Jones Beach Island is one of the outer barrier islands off the southern coast of Long Island in the U.S. state of New York.
1.  It is named for Major Thomas Jones, who first came to Long Island in 1692, where he proceeded to build the island's first brick house near Massapequa.
2.  Jones built a whaling station on Jones Island near the present site of Jones Beach State Park in 1700.
3.  Jones Beach Island is sometimes referred to as Oak Beach Island and is the former home of the infamous Oak Beach Inn.

[Oak Beach Inn]
0. The Oak Beach Inn, commonly referred to by the abbreviation OBI, was a Long Island nightclub located in Oak Beach, on Jones Beach Island near Captree State Park in the Town of Babylon, Suffolk County, New York.

[Great South Bay Bridge]
0. The Great South Bay Bridge is a bridge on the southwest side of Suffolk County, New York, on Long Island.
1.  It connects the Robert Moses Causeway from Long Island's mainland over the Great South Bay, connecting to both Captree and Jones Beach Island.
2.  It serves as access via the Robert Moses Causeway to both of the downstream crossings, the State Boat Channel Bridge and the Fire Island Inlet Bridge, also leading visitors and on-lookers to either the Fire Island Lighthouse or the Robert Moses State Park.
3.  It is the second longest bridge in the New York State, behind only the Tappan Zee Bridge.

[Gilgo-Oak Beach-Captree, New York]
0. Gilgo-Oak Beach-Captree is a former census-designated place (CDP) in Suffolk County, New York and the Town of Babylon.
1.  The population was 333 at the 2000 census.
2.  For the 2010 census the area was delineated as two CDPs: Gilgo and Oak Beach–Captree.
3.  The CDP contained several small beach communities on the barrier island along the southern edge of Long Island.
4.  In order from west to east, these include West Gilgo Beach (on the Nassau/Suffolk county border), Gilgo Beach, Cedar Beach (no residences), Oak Beach (including the Oak Island Beach Association), Oak Island and Captree Island.

[Oak Beach–Captree, New York]
0. Oak Beach–Captree is a census-designated place (CDP) in the town of Babylon in Suffolk County, New York, United States.
1.  The population was 286 at the 2010 census.
2.  Prior to the 2010 census the area was part of a larger CDP called Gilgo-Oak Beach-Captree, New York.
3.  The Oak Beach–Captree CDP consists of some small beach communities on the barrier island along the southern edge of Long Island, including Oak Beach, Oak Island, and Captree Island.

[Oak Beach, New York]
0. Oak Beach is a small community located on the eastern end of Jones Beach Island, a barrier island between the Atlantic Ocean and the Great South Bay of Long Island.
1.  The community is part of the town of Babylon in Suffolk County, New York, United States.
2.  The eastern part, the Oak Island Beach Association, is gated, whereas the western part is not.
3.  It is part of the Oak Beach–Captree census-designated place.

[South Oyster Bay]
0. South Oyster Bay or East Bay is a natural harbor along the western portion of the south shore of Long Island in New York in the United States.
1.  The harbor is formed by Jones Beach Island, a barrier island on the southern side of Long Island.
2.  It is approximately 3 mi (5 km) wide between the two islands, and approximately 15 mi (24 km) long.
3.  It links to Great South Bay on its eastern end and opens to the Atlantic Ocean through inlets on either side of Jones Beach Island.

[Great South Bay]
0. Great South Bay, actually a lagoon, is situated between Long Island and Fire Island, in the State of New York.
1.  It is approximately 45 mi long and protected from the Atlantic Ocean by Fire Island, a barrier island, as well as the eastern end of Jones Beach Island and Captree Island.

[Short Beach (New York)]
0. Short Beach is the beach on the northern shore of the western end of Jones Beach Island.
1.  The beach faces Great South Bay instead of the Atlantic Ocean, thereby providing some shelter from storm waves.
2.  Since 1851 it has been the home of a coastal lifesaving station operated (at first) by the United States Life-Saving Service and later by the United States Coast Guard.
3.  The current facility, Station Short Beach, typically does around 500 search and rescue missions each year —one of the busiest units in the Coast Guard's 1st District.
4.  The Jones Beach State Park's West End Boat Basin is also on Short Beach.
5.  The Jones Beach West End barracks of the New York State Park Police is around 200 feet south of the Short Beach shoreline.
6.  An uninhabited islet, Short Beach Island, is usually just offshore, but occasionally connects to the beach when low tide exposes sandbars to the surface.

[Lower South Bay, New York]
0. Lower South Bay, commonly called South Bay, is a hamlet on the southwest corner of Oneida Lake, Onondaga County, New York State, United States.
1.  It is opposite North Bay, and is surrounded by many islands to the west, north and east, including Geersbeck Island, Hall Island, Glosky Island, Schroeppel Island, Denmans Island and Long Island (not to be confused with Long Island, New York City).
2.  Lower South Bay also lies near the town of Cicero, about 2 miles west.
